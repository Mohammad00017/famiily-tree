📖 114 Surahs of Quran
1
✦ FIRST PROPHET ✦
آدَمُ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
Hazrat Adam (AS)
📖 Quran: 25x | First human & Prophet
✨ Created from clay, taught all names. His lineage continues through his son Shayth (Seth) — from whom all later prophets descended.
👨‍👦 Adam's 3 sons: ❌ Qabil (Cain) • Habil (Abel) • ✅ Shayth (Seth) → Ancestor of all prophets
2
✦ SECOND PROPHET ✦
إِدْرِيسُ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
Hazrat Idris (Enoch) (AS)
🏛️ Iraq (Babul) | Quran: 2x
First to write with pen, knowledgeable in astronomy & tailoring. Raised to high status.
⬇ Idris → descendant lineage → Nuh (Noah)
3
✦ GREAT PROPHET 🚢 ✦
نُوحٌ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
Hazrat Nuh (Noah) (AS)
🌊 Preached 950 years | Quran: 43x
Built the Ark; believers saved from the Great Flood. Son Sam's lineage leads to Ibrahim.
⚡ Around same era, before Ibrahim: Prophets Hud & Saleh sent to Arabian tribes ⚡
4

🏜️ To 'Aad people, Quran 7x
5
صَالِحٌ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
Hazrat Saleh (AS)

6
⭐ KHALILULLAH (FRIEND OF ALLAH) | SPLITTING POINT ⭐
إِبْرَاهِيمُ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
Hazrat Ibrahim (Abraham) (AS)
🏙️ Built Kaaba | Quran: 69x (most mentioned)
Father of Prophets. From him two great branches: Ismail (Arab lineage) & Ishaq (Bani Israel).
💡 Non-Muslims note: Abraham is the patriarch of Judaism, Christianity & Islam. From his 2 sons come the Arab and Israelite prophets.
🌟 Arab Lineage (Ismail → Quraysh → Muhammad ﷺ)
7
إِسْمَاعِيلُ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
Hazrat Ismail (Ishmael) (AS)
Built Kaaba with Ibrahim, father of the Arabs. From his descendants, Quraysh tribe emerged.
📜 Generations: Adnan → Ma'ad → Nizar → Mudhar → Ilyas → Mudrikah → Khuzaymah → Kinanah → Fihr (Quraysh) → Ghalib → Lu'ay → Ka'b → Murrah → Kilab → Qusai (guardian of Kaaba) → Abd Manaf → Hashim (Banu Hashim) → Abd al-Muttalib → Abdullah → 🌟 MUHAMMAD ﷺ
⭐ Quraysh tribe: Fihr first called "Quraysh". Prophet Muhammad ﷺ belonged to Banu Hashim clan, noble family of Mecca.
📜 Bani Israel Lineage (Ishaq → Yaqub → 12 Tribes → Great Prophets)
8
إِسْحَاقُ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
Hazrat Ishaq (Isaac) (AS)
Son of Sarah, father of the Israelite prophets.
9
يَعْقُوبُ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
Hazrat Yaqub (Jacob) (AS)
Also called Israel (Abdullah). His 12 sons became the 12 tribes of Bani Israel.
10
يُوسُفُ عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
Hazrat Yusuf (Joseph) (AS)
Surah Yusuf: Visionary, Vizier of Egypt, forgave his brothers.
⬇ After Yusuf, Bani Israel grew; Allah sent Musa, Dawud, Sulaiman, Zakariya, Yahya, Isa (peace be upon them all).
